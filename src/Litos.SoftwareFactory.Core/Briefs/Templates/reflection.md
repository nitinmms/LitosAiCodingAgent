## What makes a good lesson

- A business rule a person corrected, a project convention with a source, or implementation guidance that came up more than once.
- One instruction, at most 600 characters, that a later task can follow without this task's context. Say what to do, not what happened.
- One to eight topics: words a later task's title or request would contain when the lesson applies. Paths only when it applies to certain files.
- Each rests on one item above, cited by its id and kind.

Never propose a secret, a log or stack trace, a transient failure, a guess, a whole file, or an instruction that only applied to this task. Do not repeat a lesson the project already has.

Zero lessons is a good answer: most tasks teach nothing a later one needs. Propose at most three.

Call `submit_lessons` once, with the lessons or an empty list, and stop.
